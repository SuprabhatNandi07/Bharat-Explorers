THE UNTOUCHED EAST

EAST & NORTHEAST

Living root bridges, dense primeval mangrove forests, rolling tea estates, and beautifully preserved tribal heritage.

West Bengal

⛅ Best Time: October to March 🌿 Vibe: Himalayas, Culture & Sundarbans 🍛 Must Eat: Macher Jhol & Mishti Doi

West Bengal is one of India's most geographically diverse states, stretching from the Himalayan foothills in the north to the mangrove forests of the Sundarbans in the south. It combines colonial Kolkata, misty Darjeeling tea gardens, the forests of Dooars, terracotta temples of Bishnupur, and the world's largest mangrove delta.

📍 Major Regions & Attractions

🏛️ Kolkata (City of Joy)

Culture & Heritage: Explore the grand Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, Indian Museum, College Street bookstores, and the historic neighborhoods of North Kolkata.

🚂 The Himalayas

Darjeeling, Kalimpong & Sandakphu: Ride the UNESCO-listed Himalayan Railway, enjoy spectacular views of Mt. Kanchenjunga, and trek to Sandakphu.

🐅 Sundarbans & Dooars

Mangroves & Forests: Explore the world's largest mangrove delta searching for Royal Bengal Tigers, or visit Jaldapara and Gorumara for rhinos and elephants.

🛕 Heritage & Culture

Bishnupur & Shantiniketan: Admire exquisite terracotta temples, buy traditional Baluchari textiles, and explore the cultural legacy of Rabindranath Tagore.

🍲 Iconic Bengali Cuisine

🐟 Macher Jhol (Fish Curry) 🐟 Shorshe Ilish (Mustard Hilsa) 🍖 Kosha Mangsho (Mutton) 🫓 Luchi & Alur Dom 🌯 Kolkata Kathi Roll 🍮 Mishti Doi & Rosogolla

⚠️ Travel Circuits & Tips

• Durga Puja: Visiting Kolkata during Durga Puja (Sept/Oct) is a once-in-a-lifetime cultural spectacle, as the entire city becomes an art gallery.

• Himalayan Circuit: Darjeeling → Kalimpong → Kurseong → Sandakphu (Best for tea gardens and trekking).

Sikkim

⛅ Best Time: Mar to May, Oct to Nov 🏔️ Vibe: Himalayan Peaks & Monasteries 🍜 Must Eat: Momos, Thukpa & Phagshapa

Sikkim is a small Himalayan state with an extraordinary concentration of mountains, monasteries, forests, and alpine landscapes. Dominated by Mount Kanchenjunga (the world's third-highest mountain), Sikkim is famous for its peaceful Buddhist culture, winding mountain roads, vibrant rhododendron forests, and spectacular high-altitude destinations.

📍 Major Destinations & Landscapes

🏙️ Gangtok & Surroundings

The Hill Capital: Explore peaceful Buddhist monasteries, vibrant mountain cafés, and stunning viewpoints offering Himalayan panoramas.

🌊 High-Altitude Lakes

Tsomgo Lake & Gurudongmar: Visit the dramatic glacial Tsomgo Lake, and Gurudongmar, one of the highest and most sacred lakes in India.

🏔️ North Sikkim & Passes

Lachung, Lachen & Nathula Pass: Experience the Yumthang "Valley of Flowers", Zero Point snowscapes, and the historic Silk Road pass to Tibet.

🛕 West & South Sikkim

Pelling, Yuksom & Ravangla: Unbeatable views of Mt. Kanchenjunga, ancient ruins of Rabdentse, and giant Buddha statues.

🍲 Himalayan Comfort Food

🥟 Steamed Momos 🍜 Hearty Thukpa 🍖 Phagshapa (Pork Stew) 🥟 Sha Phaley 🧀 Chhurpi (Yak Cheese)

Meghalaya

⛅ Best Time: October to April 🌿 Vibe: Waterfalls, Caves & Root Bridges 🍖 Must Eat: Jadoh & Dohneiihong

Meghalaya, meaning "Abode of Clouds," is one of India's most spectacular northeastern states. Its dramatic landscape is shaped by extraordinary rainfall, creating dense forests, enormous waterfalls, deep limestone caves, mist-covered hills, and crystal-clear rivers.

📍 Key Destinations & Wonders

🏙️ Shillong

The Scotland of the East: Explore the state's lively, music-loving hill capital surrounded by pine forests, Ward's Lake, and cafes.

🌧️ Cherrapunji & Mawsynram

The Wettest Places on Earth: Discover dramatic cliffs, plunging waterfalls, and deep cave systems carved by centuries of heavy rain.

🌉 Living Root Bridges

Nongriat: Trek deep into the jungle to cross the world-famous Double-Decker Living Root Bridge, woven naturally from rubber fig trees.

🌊 Dawki & Mawlynnong

Crystal Rivers: Boat on the transparent, glass-like waters of the Umngot River, and visit Asia's cleanest village.

🍲 Khasi & Jaintia Delicacies

🍚 Jadoh (Rice & Meat) 🍖 Dohneiihong (Pork with Black Sesame) 🌿 Tungrymbai (Fermented Soybeans) 🎋 Bamboo Shoot Dishes 🥞 Pukhlein (Sweet Rice Bread)

Assam

⛅ Best Time: October to April 🌿 Vibe: Tea Gardens, Rivers & Wildlife 🍛 Must Eat: Masor Tenga & Pitha

Assam is the gateway to Northeast India and a lush land shaped by the mighty Brahmaputra River. It is internationally famous for its sprawling tea plantations and Kaziranga National Park, home to the world's largest population of Greater One-Horned Rhinoceroses.

📍 Major Regions & Attractions

🐅 Kaziranga & Wildlife

Rhinos & Foothill Ecology: Embark on safaris in Kaziranga, Manas, Pobitora, and Orang National Parks to spot rhinos and tigers.

🍵 Tea Country

Dibrugarh, Jorhat & Tinsukia: Stay in heritage plantation bungalows and explore endless emerald tea estates.

🏝️ Majuli Island

World's Largest River Island: Experience the unique neo-Vaishnavite culture, traditional mask-making, and monasteries in the Brahmaputra.

🛕 Guwahati & Temples

Kamakhya Temple: Visit the deeply sacred Kamakhya Temple, and take a boat to Umananda (Peacock Island) on the Brahmaputra.

🍲 Assamese Comfort Food

🐟 Masor Tenga (Sour Fish Curry) 🥣 Khar (Alkaline Palate Cleanser) 🥔 Aloo Pitika (Mashed Potatoes) 🥞 Pitha (Rice Sweets) 🍵 Assam Black Tea

Arunachal Pradesh

⛅ Best Time: October to April 🏔️ Vibe: The Last Great Frontier 🍜 Must Eat: Thukpa, Zan & Apong

Known as the "Land of Dawn-Lit Mountains," Arunachal Pradesh is India's wildest and least explored frontier. It offers winding mountain roads through Tawang, massive ancient Buddhist monasteries, high-altitude lakes, and dense pristine forests hiding remote Himalayan valleys.

📍 Major Destinations & Valleys

🛕 Tawang & Sela Pass

Buddhist Heritage & High Passes: Home to the massive Tawang Monastery (India's largest), the frozen Sela Lake, and dramatic snow-covered Himalayan passes.

🌾 Ziro Valley

Apatani Culture: A picturesque pine-clad valley famous for its terraced rice fields, unique Apatani tribal heritage, and the annual Ziro Music Festival.

🐅 Namdapha National Park

Untouched Wilderness: A sprawling biodiversity hotspot harboring snow leopards, clouded leopards, tigers, and red pandas within dense rainforests.

🍲 Tribal & Himalayan Food

🍜 Thukpa 🥟 Momos 🌾 Zan (Millet Porridge) 🌿 Bamboo Shoot Curries

Manipur

⛅ Best Time: October to March 🌊 Vibe: Floating Islands & Green Valleys 🍛 Must Eat: Eromba & Singju

Described as the "Jewel of the Northeast," Manipur is a land of rolling blue hills, classical dance, and deeply preserved traditions. It is world-famous for Loktak Lake's floating phumdis (islands) and the endangered Sangai deer.

📍 Major Destinations & Attractions

🌊 Loktak Lake & Keibul Lamjao

Floating National Park: The world's only floating national park, composed of circular floating biomasses (phumdis), home to the dancing Sangai deer.

🏙️ Imphal & Kangla Fort

Heritage & History: The vibrant capital city featuring the historic Kangla Fort, Ima Keithel (a massive market run entirely by women), and WWII memorials.

🍲 Traditional Manipuri Food

🐟 Eromba (Fish & Veg Mash) 🥗 Singju (Spicy Salad) 🍲 Chamthong (Vegetable Stew) 🍚 Black Rice Pudding

Tripura

⛅ Best Time: October to March 🏰 Vibe: Palaces & Bamboo Trails 🍛 Must Eat: Mui Borok & Chakhwi

Tripura is a hidden cultural treasure of the Northeast, boasting grand royal palaces reflecting its princely past, ancient rock-cut carvings hidden deep in forests, and expansive bamboo groves.

📍 Major Destinations & Attractions

🏰 Agartala & Ujjayanta Palace

Royal Heritage: The gleaming white Ujjayanta Palace, sprawling gardens, and the vibrant local culture of the state capital.

🌊 Neermahal (The Water Palace)

Architectural Marvel: A breathtaking red-and-white palace floating in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake, blending Hindu and Mughal architectural styles.

🪨 Unakoti Rock Carvings

Ancient Mysteries: Massive, centuries-old rock-cut sculptures of Hindu deities carved directly into a forested hillside.

🍲 Tripuri Food

🍲 Mui Borok (Traditional Cuisine) 🎋 Chakhwi (Bamboo Shoot Dish) 🐟 Mosdeng (Spicy Chutney)

Odisha

⛅ Best Time: October to March 🌊 Vibe: Temples, Beaches & Tribal Heritage 🍛 Must Eat: Dalma & Chhena Poda

Odisha is one of India's great cultural treasures, combining magnificent Kalinga temple architecture, long stretches of coastline, deep tribal traditions, classical Odissi dance, and rich coastal cuisine.

📍 Major Destinations & Nature

🛕 The Golden Triangle

Bhubaneswar, Puri & Konark: Explore the "City of Temples," the deeply sacred Jagannath Temple in Puri, and the colossal, chariot-shaped Konark Sun Temple.

🐦 Chilika Lake

Dolphins & Migratory Birds: Asia's largest brackish water lagoon, famous for spotting Irrawaddy dolphins and millions of winter migratory birds.

🌊 Coastal Beaches

Gopalpur, Chandipur & Chandrabhaga: Relax on quiet beaches, or witness the bizarre "vanishing sea" phenomenon at Chandipur.

🐅 Forests & Wildlife

Simlipal, Satkosia & Bhitarkanika: Trek through dense tiger reserves, boat through crocodile-infested mangroves, and explore the Satkosia river gorge.

🍲 Coastal Odia Cuisine

🍛 Dalma (Lentil & Vegetable Stew) 🍚 Pakhala Bhata (Water Rice) 🐟 Macha Ghanta (Fish Head Curry) 🍰 Chhena Poda (Roasted Cheese Sweet) 🍮 Rasabali

Bihar

⛅ Best Time: October to March 🛕 Vibe: Ancient Civilizations & Buddhism 🍛 Must Eat: Litti Chokha & Thekua

Bihar is one of India's most historically profound regions, serving as the cradle of ancient civilization, Buddhism, and Jainism. Once the heartland of the powerful Magadha empire, it is home to the world's earliest universities (Nalanda) and the exact spot where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment (Bodh Gaya).

📍 Major Destinations & Heritage

🪷 Bodh Gaya & Gaya

The Center of Buddhism: Sit beneath the sacred Bodhi Tree at the Mahabodhi Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site and Buddhism's holiest pilgrimage destination.

📚 Ancient Universities

Nalanda & Vikramshila: Wander through the massive, excavated red-brick ruins of Nalanda, one of the greatest centers of learning in the ancient world.

⛰️ Rajgir & Pawapuri

Hills & Jain Heritage: Explore ancient caves, hot springs, the Vishwa Shanti Stupa in Rajgir, and the sacred Jain Jal Mandir in Pawapuri.

🎨 Madhubani & Kesaria

Art & Giant Stupas: Explore the birthplace of the vibrant Mithila (Madhubani) painting tradition, and see the massive, multi-tiered Buddhist stupa at Kesaria.

🍲 Rustic Bihari Cuisine

🫓 Litti Chokha (Stuffed Wheat Balls) 🥞 Sattu Paratha 🍖 Champaran Meat (Ahuna Mutton) 🍹 Sattu Sharbat 🍪 Thekua (Crunchy Sweet) 🥞 Malpua
