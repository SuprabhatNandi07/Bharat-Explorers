India Through Time

5,000 years of history shaping the modern subcontinent.

2500 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization: One of the world's oldest advanced urban societies flourishes.

320 BCE

Mauryan Empire: Chandragupta Maurya and Ashoka unite almost the entire subcontinent.

1526 CE

The Mughal Era: A golden age of art, culture, and architecture, including the Taj Mahal.

1947 CE

Independence: India breaks free from British rule to become the world's largest democracy.

A Nation Served on a Plate

Discover the regional food traditions of India.

🍛

Bengali Cuisine

A delicate yet flavorful tradition known for mustard oil, freshwater fish, rice, subtle spices, and iconic sweets such as Rasgulla, Sandesh, and Mishti Doi.

🥘

Rajasthani Cuisine

A hearty desert cuisine built around gram flour, lentils, ghee, and dried ingredients, celebrated through Dal Baati Churma, Gatte ki Sabzi, and Ker Sangri.

🥥

Kerala Cuisine

A tropical culinary tradition featuring coconut, curry leaves, tamarind, seafood, and aromatic spices, with Kerala Sadya, Appam, and Puttu among its signature dishes.

🍚

Hyderabadi Cuisine

A rich Indo-Islamic culinary tradition famous for slow-cooked dishes, fragrant basmati rice, saffron, and aromatic spices, best represented by Hyderabadi Biryani and Haleem.

🫓

Punjabi Cuisine

Bold and comforting food characterized by tandoori cooking, butter, cream, ghee, and robust spices, featuring Butter Chicken, Chole Bhature, Sarson da Saag, and Makki di Roti.

🍽️

Gujarati Cuisine

A predominantly vegetarian cuisine famous for its distinctive sweet, savory, and tangy balance, with Dhokla, Thepla, Undhiyu, Fafda, and Gujarati Thali.

🌶️

Maharashtrian Cuisine

A diverse cuisine ranging from fiery Kolhapuri preparations to comforting street foods, featuring Misal Pav, Vada Pav, Puran Poli, and Bharli Vangi.

🐟

Goan Cuisine

A coastal fusion of Indian and Portuguese influences featuring seafood, coconut, kokum, vinegar, and chilies, with Goan Fish Curry, Prawn Balchão, and Vindaloo as favorites.

🍲

Tamil Cuisine

A traditional South Indian cuisine centered around rice, lentils, tamarind, coconut, and aromatic spices, featuring Idli, Dosa, Sambar, Rasam, and Pongal.

🍗

Chettinad Cuisine

A fiery cuisine from Tamil Nadu known for freshly roasted spices, black pepper, fennel, curry leaves, and rich meat preparations such as Chettinad Chicken.

🌶️

Andhra Cuisine

Famous for its bold heat, tangy flavors, gongura leaves, tamarind, and red chilies, with dishes such as Gongura Pachadi, Andhra Chicken, Pulihora, and spicy Andhra meals.

🍛

Karnataka Cuisine

A wonderfully diverse tradition ranging from Udupi vegetarian food to spicy regional dishes, featuring Bisi Bele Bath, Mysore Masala Dosa, Ragi Mudde, and Mysore Pak.

🥘

Odia Cuisine

Known for simple preparation, balanced spices, mustard, vegetables, and seafood, with traditional favorites including Dalma, Pakhala Bhata, Machha Besara, and Chhena Poda.

🐟

Assamese Cuisine

A light and earthy cuisine that makes extensive use of rice, freshwater fish, herbs, bamboo shoots, and fermented ingredients, with Masor Tenga and Khar among its specialties.

🥟

Sikkimese Cuisine

A Himalayan food culture influenced by Tibetan and Nepali traditions, featuring steaming hot momos, thukpa, fermented vegetables, and hearty noodle-based dishes.

🌶️

Naga Cuisine

An earthy and intensely flavored cuisine known for smoked meats, fermented ingredients, bamboo shoots, herbs, and fiery Naga chilies, especially in smoked pork preparations.

🍲

Manipuri Cuisine

A distinctive cuisine built around rice, seasonal vegetables, herbs, fermented fish, and local ingredients, with Eromba, Singju, and Chamthong among its popular dishes.

🥬

Mizo Cuisine

Simple yet flavorful northeastern cooking centered on rice, vegetables, herbs, bamboo shoots, and meat, with Bai being one of Mizoram's best-known traditional dishes.

🍖

Tripuri Cuisine

The traditional cuisine of Tripura, known as Mui Borok, features rice, vegetables, herbs, fermented fish, and bamboo shoots, with Chakhwi and Mosdeng forming popular preparations.

🍜

Arunachali Cuisine

A Himalayan and tribal food tradition featuring rice, smoked meat, bamboo shoots, fermented foods, and warming soups such as Thukpa.

🥩

Meghalaya Cuisine

Rich in indigenous Khasi, Jaintia, and Garo traditions, this cuisine highlights rice, pork, bamboo shoots, and local herbs, with Jadoh and Dohneiih as notable dishes.

🍖

Kashmiri Cuisine

An aromatic culinary tradition famous for saffron, fennel, dry fruits, yogurt, and carefully balanced spices, featuring Rogan Josh, Yakhni, and the elaborate Wazwan feast.

🍜

Ladakhi Cuisine

High-altitude Himalayan food designed for a cold climate, featuring barley, wheat, vegetables, yak or meat preparations, and warming dishes such as Thukpa and Momos.

🥘

Himachali Cuisine

A mountain cuisine centered around lentils, grains, yogurt, and locally available ingredients, with the traditional Dham feast, Siddu, and Chana Madra among its specialties.

🥬

Uttarakhand Cuisine

Simple Himalayan cooking featuring millets, lentils, local herbs, and earthy flavors, with dishes such as Kafuli, Aloo ke Gutke, Bhatt ki Churdkani, and Jhangore ki Kheer.

🍢

Awadhi Cuisine

The refined culinary tradition of Lucknow, celebrated for slow cooking, fragrant spices, delicate kebabs, and rich gravies, including Galouti Kebab, Lucknowi Biryani, and Korma.

🍗

Mughlai Cuisine

A luxurious North Indian tradition influenced by the Mughal royal kitchens, known for rich gravies, aromatic spices, nuts, dried fruits, and dishes such as Korma, Kebabs, and Butter Chicken.

🥘

Bihari Cuisine

A rustic and satisfying cuisine built around sattu, wheat, rice, mustard oil, and seasonal vegetables, best known for Litti Chokha, Sattu Paratha, and Thekua.

🍚

Jharkhandi Cuisine

An indigenous food tradition emphasizing rice, millets, leafy greens, forest produce, and simple spices, with Dhuska, Rugra, Chilka Roti, and Thekua among its specialties.

🌾

Chhattisgarhi Cuisine

A largely rice-based cuisine featuring indigenous grains, lentils, herbs, and locally available ingredients, with Fara, Chila, Angakar Roti, and Aamat as popular dishes.

🥜

Madhya Pradesh Cuisine

A diverse central Indian cuisine combining wheat, lentils, spices, and street-food traditions, famous for Poha, Bhutte ka Kees, Dal Bafla, and Bhopali-style dishes.

🫓

Haryanvi Cuisine

A rustic and wholesome cuisine reflecting the agricultural culture of Haryana, featuring wheat, bajra, dairy, vegetables, and dishes such as Bajra Khichdi, Kadhi, and Churma.

🥔

Kumaoni Cuisine

A traditional Himalayan cuisine from the Kumaon region, emphasizing simple ingredients, lentils, grains, and local herbs, with Aloo ke Gutke, Bhatt ki Churdkani, and Ras-Bhaat.

🍛

Sindhi Cuisine

A flavorful culinary tradition known for tangy gravies, lentils, vegetables, and wheat-based breads, featuring Sindhi Kadhi, Sai Bhaji, Dal Pakwan, and Koki.
